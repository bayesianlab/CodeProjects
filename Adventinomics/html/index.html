Our Mission

We exist to fulfill the three angels messages of Revelation 14. This means proclaiming who the Creator is and how to be right with him, warning mankind to leave Babylon, and telling as many people as possible what the mark of the beast is and how to avoid receiving the mark. How the "whole world will wonder after the beast" involves the interplay of many different factors ranging from economics to culture. For this reason we aim to give you our perspective in how all these events are leading toward the fulfillment of this prophecy.
